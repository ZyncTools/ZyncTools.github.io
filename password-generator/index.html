About Password Generator

Generate strong random passwords or memorable passphrases. Password Generator needs no input file — choose your settings and it generates the result straight away.

Your files stay on your device. ZyncTools has no upload step: the file is read in your browser, processed there, and handed straight back to you. There is no account, no watermark and no size cap beyond what your own device can hold.

Settings. Password Generator gives you control over password style, length, uppercase a–z, lowercase a–z, digits 0–9, symbols !@#$…, avoid look-alike characters (0/o, 1/l/i) and also exclude these characters, plus 6 more.

Also known as: password, random, secure, passphrase, generator.

How to use Password Generator
  1. Open the tool. Open Password Generator. There is nothing to upload — it works from the settings alone.
  2. Choose your settings. Adjust password style, length, uppercase a–z and the other 11 settings to suit what you need.
  3. Generate. Press Generate. The result appears immediately and updates whenever you change a setting.
  4. Save the result. Copy the output, or press Download to save it. Nothing is kept once you close the tab.
Frequently asked questions
Are these passwords actually random?

Yes. They come from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographic random source, not Math.random. Generation happens locally and no password is ever transmitted or logged.

Should I use a random password or a passphrase?

A passphrase of four or more words is easier to type and remember while being just as hard to guess. Use random characters where a site imposes a length limit, since entropy per character is higher.

Are my files uploaded when I use Password Generator?

No. Password Generator runs entirely inside your browser. Your file is read locally, processed on your own device, and handed straight back to you. You can confirm this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, watch the Network tab, and run the tool — no request carries your data anywhere.

Is Password Generator free?

Yes, completely. No account, no sign-up, no watermark on your output, and no cap on how many times you can use it. The project is open source under the AGPL licence.

Does Password Generator work offline?

Mostly. The site caches itself after your first visit, so it keeps working without a connection. Tools that need a specialised library — PDF editing, OCR, QR codes, MP3 encoding — need a connection the first time you run them, then that library is cached too.
